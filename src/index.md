I am a PhD in École Polytechnique at the LIX lab under the supervision of Lutz Strassburger and Dominik Kirst and I am part of the PARTOUT team. Previously I did a six months stage at PICUBE at IRIF. \\ I did my masters in Mathematics at the University of Turin (UNITO) specializing in logic. \\ My academic interests are proof theory, type theory, formalization, modal logic and intuitionistic modal logic. I also like philosophy of the practice of mathematics. \\ I have been working on a Rocq formalization of Kripke semantics together with my supervisor Dominik Kirst.
