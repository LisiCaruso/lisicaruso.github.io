I am a PhD in École Polytechnique at the LIX lab under the supervision of Lutz Strassburger and Dominik Kirst and I am part of the PARTOUT team. Previously I did a six months stage at PICUBE at IRIF. 

I did my masters in Mathematics at the University of Turin (UNITO) specializing in logic. 

My academic interests are proof theory, type theory, formalization, modal logic and intuitionistic modal logic. I also like philosophy of the practice of mathematics. 

I have been working on a Rocq formalization of Kripke semantics together with Dominik Kirst.

# Teaching

I am teaching the TD for the proof theory course of LMFI for the academic year 2026/2027.

Biology and Natural Sciences, 40h); Basics of Coding and General Mathematics (Dep of Economics, 40h + 100h).
